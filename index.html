How to Link

Follow these steps to connect your Minecraft and Discord accounts.

01

Join Minecastle

Join play.revoksmp.fun and find the Link Account option.

02

Click Link Account

Open the linking option and follow the instructions shown in Minecraft chat.

03

Use Discord

In Discord, open #commands and use /link.

04

Copy the Code

The bot gives you a unique activation code. Copy it.

05

Enter the Code

Return to Minecraft and use /link <code>.

06

Done ✓

Your accounts are linked successfully.
